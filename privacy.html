Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

This policy covers the "Artificial Apparition" app: a private production tool used by the owner of the Artificial Apparition YouTube channel to publish and track that channel's videos. It is not offered to the public. The only Google account it is used with is the channel owner's own.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Google data the app uses, and why

  • Uploading and managing videos (YouTube upload and manage scopes): to upload finished episodes to the owner's channel, set their thumbnails, captions and playlist entries, set each video's visibility, and mark them as containing synthetic (AI-generated) media.
  • Channel details: the channel's ID and name, to confirm the app is connected to the right channel before it uploads anything.
  • YouTube Analytics (read-only): views, watch time, subscribers, likes, audience retention and traffic sources for the owner's channel and videos, shown in the owner's private dashboard.

The app does not read, collect or process data about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

Where data is kept

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted so that only the owner's Windows account can read it.
  • Analytics are fetched when the owner opens the dashboard, held briefly in memory to avoid repeated requests, and not saved or exported.
  • Records of the app's own uploads (video IDs, titles, dates) are kept on the owner's computer and in the owner's private code repository.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The AI services that help make the episodes (story writing, narration and animation) receive only the episode's own story material, never data obtained from Google or YouTube.

Artificial Apparition's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through Google's security settings. Revoking access stops all use of the account immediately. To have the stored token and records deleted, email the address below; they are deleted within 7 days.
