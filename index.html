Artificial Apparition

Artificial Apparition is a YouTube horror channel. Each episode is an animated, narrated retelling of a piece of folklore, a cryptid legend or a reported encounter, told as a dramatised story rather than a claim that any of it is true. The visuals and narration are made with AI tools.

The Artificial Apparition app

"Artificial Apparition" is also the name of the private production tool the channel's owner uses to publish episodes. It signs in with Google only to manage the owner's own YouTube channel: uploading finished episodes with their thumbnails, captions and playlist entries, setting each video's visibility, and reading the channel's YouTube Analytics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
